# Billing

## Free during beta

PerpsCopier is **free during the beta**. Settings → Account shows your plan as **Free beta**, and copying works with no plan.

## When billing starts

When billing starts, a **PerpsCopier plan** is needed to copy. See [perpscopier.com](https://perpscopier.com/) for current pricing.

### "The free beta ends on …"

Before the beta ends, the app shows a banner: **"The free beta ends on …"**. Copying keeps working until then.

- **Start 5-day free trial** (or **Subscribe**) opens Stripe Checkout in your browser.
- **Later** hides the banner for a day.

### 5-day free trial

New plans start with a **5-day free trial**. You pay nothing for 5 days and can cancel any time in **Settings → Manage plan**.

### If you have no plan

Once billing is on, **copying pauses** without a plan. The banner says so and has a button to start the trial or subscribe. **Open positions are not touched.**

After you pay in the browser, the app unlocks by itself a few seconds later. If it doesn't, press **Check again**.

## Manage plan

**Settings → Account → Manage plan** opens the **Stripe customer portal** in your browser. There you can:

- Change your card
- See invoices
- Cancel

Changes made there show in the app soon after.

## Offline

The app checks your PerpsCopier account about every 30 minutes. If it can't reach perpscopier.com, copying keeps working for up to **72 hours**, so a network blip never stops you mid-trade. After 72 hours without a check, the app signs out and copying stops. Open positions are not touched.
