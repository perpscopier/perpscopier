# Settings

Open **Settings** in the app's menu. It shows the app version and has these sections.

## Startup

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Start with Windows** | Off | Opens PerpsCopier in the tray when you log in to your computer |
| **Keep copying after a restart** | On | Copying stays on until you press Stop, even after a restart or a disconnect |

With **Keep copying after a restart** on, copying turns back on by itself after the app restarts (for example after an update). You get a notification when it does.

## Sounds

**Fill sound** plays a sound when any of your accounts gets a fill. Pick a sound, or **off**. Press **Buy** or **Sell** to hear it. A buy sounds a little higher than a sell.

## Account

Shows the email of your PerpsCopier account and your plan, for example **Free beta**. One computer per account.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Manage plan** | Opens the Stripe customer portal in your browser: card, invoices, cancel. Shown once you have a plan. See [Billing](billing.md). |
| **Log out** | Logs out of PerpsCopier. Copying stops on this computer. Open positions are not touched. |

## Updates

Shows whether you have the latest version, a download in progress, or an update ready. **Check now** checks right away. **Restart to update** installs a downloaded update. See [Updates](updates.md).

## Shortcuts and files

| Shortcut / button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl+Shift+K** | **Emergency stop**, from anywhere in Windows. Stops copying immediately. **Positions stay open.** |
| **Flatten** / **Flatten all** (Cockpit) | Close positions at market. Copying stays on. See [Risk limits](risk-limits.md#stopping-and-closing). |
| **Stop & close all** (Cockpit) | Closes the Vest leader's positions and the Vest positions the app copied, and stops copying. Tradovate / Rithmic positions are not touched. |
| **Open data folder** | Opens the folder with your settings, Copy Log and position map |

> **Ctrl+Shift+K does not close anything.** It only stops copying. To close positions, use **Flatten** or **Stop & close all** on the Cockpit, or close them at your broker.

Your settings and broker logins stay on this computer.
