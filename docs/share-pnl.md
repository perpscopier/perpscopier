# Share P&L

**Share P&L** makes an image of today's P&L that you can post on X, Discord or Telegram. It works for all your accounts together or for one account.

## Make a P&L image

1. Open the **Cockpit**.
2. Press **Share P&L** at the top.
3. Choose the options:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Accounts** | **All accounts** together, or one account (each is listed with its role and platform) |
| **Include open trades** | Adds today's open P&L to the closed P&L |
| **Show $ amounts** | Shows dollar amounts. Turn it off to share without dollar figures. |

4. Press **Copy image** and paste it into X, Discord or Telegram. Or press **Save image…** to save it as a file.

## What the image shows

- Today's P&L for the accounts you chose: closed P&L, plus open P&L if ticked.
- The percentage change against today's starting balance (balance now minus what was closed today).
- A **QR code** to your referral link, so people who scan it are credited to you. If you have no referral link yet, it points to perpscopier.com. See [Refer a friend](settings.md#refer-a-friend).

The numbers are the same ones the Cockpit shows.
